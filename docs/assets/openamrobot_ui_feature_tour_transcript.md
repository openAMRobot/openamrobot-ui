# OpenAMRobot UI Feature Tour — Transcript

**Duration:** approximately 84 seconds  
**Language:** English

Welcome to Open A-M Robot U-I—one dashboard for operating and understanding
your ROS 2 robot.

From the map, you can drive manually, send navigation goals, manage waypoints,
and dock. Build and edit reusable waypoint routes for each map, and easily
create, switch, rename, and organize your robot maps.

Blockly makes it simple to build reliable robot routines visually. You can
schedule routine browser-based actions and combine waypoints, waiting periods,
and docking into multi-step missions.

Monitor the camera, battery, robot movement, and overall system status. Explore
the robot model, its joints, and helpful live overlays. You can also register
and manage external hardware and network-connected devices.

Quickly check robot readiness, battery levels, connected devices, and faults.
Track distance, uptime, navigation results, and docking performance.

Record and replay ROS bags to simplify debugging and demonstrations. Review
important navigation, battery, docking, and safety events. You can also inspect
system logs and monitor live ROS topics directly from your browser.

View and update selected Nav2 runtime parameters, manage your robot profiles,
and choose which robot you want to control.

Start safely in Demo Mode—then self-host the complete interface when you’re
ready.
